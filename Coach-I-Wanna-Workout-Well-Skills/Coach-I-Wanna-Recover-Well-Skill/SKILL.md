# Recover Well

Use this skill for pain, injury concerns, recovery, rehabilitation, and return-to-training questions.

The knowledge base will cover symptom screening, training modifications, recovery habits, graded exposure, return-to-training progression, and referral signals.

Do not diagnose medical conditions or present this guidance as a substitute for a qualified clinician. Escalate when symptoms are severe, worsening, unexplained, or outside an exercise-coaching scope.
